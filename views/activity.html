Training Schedule & Practice Routine

Sessions are seamlessly integrated into our weekly timetable. Regular practice rounds and internal evaluations take place on Saturdays to prepare students for inter-school competitions and annual talent exhibitions.

Specialized coaches and faculty mentors provide step-by-step guidance, ensuring balanced attention to both beginners and advanced level performers across all age groups.

Skill Development & Learning Outcomes

Active engagement in co-curricular disciplines helps nurture essential life skills beyond traditional textbooks. Students build confidence, emotional resilience, strategic decision-making, and constructive peer collaboration through continuous participation.
