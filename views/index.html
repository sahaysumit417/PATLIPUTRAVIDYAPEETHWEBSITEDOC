Welcome to Our Campus

Welcome to the Patliputra Vidyapeeth Family. Every child enters our campus with a spark of potential. Our commitment at Patliputra Vidyapeeth is to turn that spark into a brilliant flame of knowledge, character, and leadership.

We look at education through a holistic lens. Whether it is the focused silence inside our next-gen computer terminals, the analytical discoveries in our advanced laboratories, or the triumphant spirit showcased on our sports fields—we celebrate every dimension of development. We nurture critical thinkers who respect tradition, embrace modern science, and possess the empathy to create a better world.

Our Mission

The mission of Patliputra Vidyapeeth is to ensure sound infrastructural facilities in the field of education, education & research. Our aim is to provide quality health education for qualified professional support to the Medical and Rehab professional, particularly from rural poor families who are taught and trained here in the socio-rural environment cutting across the barriers of rural and urban area and alleviating the root of inequalities incidental to rich and poor, caste and class and other related circumstances.

Our Vision

Education means an all round development of a child. Teacher is a philosopher, guide and a friend. Indeed, we have experienced and qualified teachers who dedicate their life for this noble cause. "Teaching is the noblest profession of the world". We believe that teaching-learning process should be a process of discovery. Our school ensures both physical and mental growth of the students through training. Patliputra Vidyapeeth inculcates the spirit of patriotism, sharing, caring, cooperation and coordination.
